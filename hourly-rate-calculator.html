How to calculate your freelance hourly rate

A freelance hourly rate should cover both the income you want to earn and the costs of running your freelance business. It should also account for the fact that not every hour you work can be charged to a client.

This calculator estimates your rate using your desired annual income, business expenses, working time and estimated billable time.

Simple formula:

Minimum hourly rate = (Desired yearly income + yearly business expenses) ÷ total billable hours

Why billable hours matter

Freelancers often spend part of their working week on activities that cannot be directly charged to a client. These can include finding new clients, preparing proposals, answering emails, managing invoices, learning new skills and handling administrative work.

For this reason, your total working hours and your billable hours are not always the same. The billable-time percentage in this calculator helps account for that difference.

Freelance rates in India

Freelance rates in India can vary significantly depending on your skill, experience, industry, client location, project complexity and demand for your services. A beginner working with local clients may price very differently from an experienced freelancer serving international businesses.

Instead of copying someone else's hourly rate, use your own income goal, costs and available working time to calculate a sustainable starting point.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate in India?

Add your desired yearly income and yearly business expenses, then divide the total by your expected annual billable hours. This gives you a starting minimum hourly rate.

What is a good freelance hourly rate in India?

There is no single rate that is right for every freelancer. Your rate should reflect your skills, experience, niche, expenses, client market and income goal.

What are billable hours?

Billable hours are the hours you can charge directly to a client. Client work is usually billable, while activities such as marketing, administration and finding new clients may not be.

Should I include taxes in my freelance rate?

Taxes and other statutory costs can affect the amount you need to earn. Consider your own tax situation and professional requirements when setting your final rate.

Can I charge international clients in USD?

Yes. Freelancers in India may work with clients in India or overseas. You can use the currency selector above to estimate your rate in different currencies.

This calculator provides an estimate and is not financial or tax advice. Consider taxes, holidays, sick days, unpaid administrative time, payment fees and other business costs when deciding your final rate.
